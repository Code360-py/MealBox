About MealBox

Recipes, cooking methods, and food discovery — made simple.

Our Mission

MealBox is a lightweight recipe browser built on top of the public TheMealDB API. The goal is simple: help you find a dish, see what it takes to make it, and save the ones you love — without ads, popups, or clutter.

What You Can Do
Search recipes
Look up dishes by name from a 1200+ catalog, with recent-search history saved locally.
Browse by category
Beef, Chicken, Seafood, Dessert, Vegetarian, and more — filter with one tap.
Save favorites
Bookmark any recipe to your personal collection. Available on any device you log in from.
Full ingredient lists
Step-by-step instructions with exact measurements for every dish.

Data Source

All recipes, images, categories, and instructions come from TheMealDB — a free, open recipe database maintained by the community. MealBox does not store or claim ownership of this content.
